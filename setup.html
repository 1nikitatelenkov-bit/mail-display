Mail DisplayDEMO

Set up Mail Display for your Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses in three steps. This is the phase-1 demo build: nothing here connects to a real account yet — the sign-in backend arrives in phase 2.

STEP 1

Add your email accounts

In the finished app you will sign in to each account here on your phone, once. Your mail stays separate per account — the glasses show one account at a time, with a switcher like the Gmail app.

Demo state only: “Add” below just adds a demo row on this phone so you can see the flow. No sign-in page opens and no real connection is made — real OAuth sign-in arrives with the phase-2 backend.
STEP 2

Your free Gemini key

Mail Display uses only the free Gemini API — no paid AI. Get your own free key: open Google AI Studio, sign in with any Google account, and tap “Create API key”. Then paste it here.

Demo note: in this build the key is stored only in this browser, on this phone (localStorage) and is never sent anywhere. In phase 2 it will be stored encrypted on the backend instead. One honest heads-up: on Google’s free tier, what the AI sees may be used by Google to improve its products — that is the trade-off of free.
